# Problem definition
**Business question:** which observable characteristics distinguish Indian streaming originals that launch to strong public attention, and how can that inform content development?

**Unit:** one title (a film, or a series at its first-season premiere) released as an original on an Indian streaming service between January 2018 and October 2026.

**Outcome:** no platform publishes comparable viewership. The study uses a stated proxy: English-Wikipedia pageviews in the 28 days from release ("launch attention"). It is time-stamped, free of platform self-reporting and available for every platform, but it is not viewership.

**Questions answered**
1. How does launch attention vary by language, genre, format and platform?
2. How has the genre mix and attention changed by release year?
3. Which characteristics remain associated with attention after controls?
4. Can a strong launch be predicted from information available before release?
5. Which language x genre combinations draw more attention than their share of supply suggests?

**Not answered:** actual viewing, completion, subscriber impact, or anything causal.
